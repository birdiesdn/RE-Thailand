## Findings

**Taling Chan now shows an 8× internal spread.** Three plots, one district:
฿6,188/m² (1 rai), ฿15,000/m² (the 7,888 m² estate site), and ฿49,505/m²
(404 m²). The two cheap ones are the two large ones. This is the same pattern
Bang Na showed last week, and it is now firm enough to state as a rule for this
register: **in Bangkok, small road-fronting parcels are priced as frontage, not
as land, and district averages conceal it.** Any comparison that does not
control for parcel size and road position is noise.

**Pom Prap Sattru Phai is cheap for a reason worth checking.** ฿35,000/m²
inside the MRT ring looks like an anomaly against Thong Lo at ฿162,500/m². It
probably is not one: the district is conservation-sensitive old town —
shophouse fabric, narrow lanes, height and access constraints. The constraint
question matters more than the price here, and it cannot be answered from a
listing.

## A source caveat

Dot Property's aggregate page quotes an average Bangkok land price of
฿162,690,752 (≈฿25,449/m²) "as of 1 Oct 2026" across 2,806 plots. The page
also carries a 488-day age stamp, so that date is not trustworthy. **The figure
is not recorded in the tracker** and should not be cited as a current market
average.
