## Three findings worth acting on

1. **The Prawet plot backing the AP project is the clearest value case added
   this month.** AP Thailand ran its own site selection and land acquisition on
   the adjoining parcel; buying next door inherits that work. ฿20M for a full
   rai is also within reach of a first project.

2. **Bang Na is not one market.** The register now holds Bang Na at
   ฿50,000/sq.wah and at ฿250,000/sq.wah. Frontage on the Bangna-Trad spine
   prices like the inner city; interior sois do not. Any Bang Na comparison
   that does not distinguish the two is meaningless — including the
   ฿119,624/sq.wah district median this tracker quoted back in July.

3. **The Lat Krabang gap.** Two plots, same district: ฿2,875/m² and ฿8,838/m².
   The cheaper one is also the larger. Flight path, access, or title will
   explain it, and none of those shows up in a listing.
