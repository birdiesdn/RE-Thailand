### A note on one source figure

A search result paired "7 rai 2 ngaan 85 sq wah" with the ฿6,110,000 price and
a ฿19,238/m² rate. Those three cannot describe one plot — ฿6.11M over 12,340 m²
is ฿495/m². The ฿6.11M / ฿77,000-per-wah / ฿19,238-per-m² figures are mutually
consistent for a ~79 sq.wah plot, so that is what the register records. The
7-rai figure appears to belong to a different listing and was not carried over.
