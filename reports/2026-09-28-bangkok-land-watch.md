# Bangkok Land Watch — 28 September 2026 (weekly run)

Covers four weeks (7–28 September). The 14 and 21 September runs each gathered
data but were cut off before writing, and the 7 September publish never ran, so
this entry consolidates all of it and the tracker page is republished with
everything from 7 September onward. Web-search run — the egress block is
unchanged (see below). Gmail: no portal alert emails. Google Drive: no KML/KMZ
tracings, so all footprint gauges remain assumed squares.

## Network block — unchanged, and now well characterised

Re-tested each week. Every attempt fails identically:

connect_rejected — gateway answered 403 to CONNECT (policy denial)

own diagnostics, affecting both the six listing portals and
`server.arcgisonline.com`. Retrying is explicitly the wrong response. **Aerial
snapshots remain impossible to build until an admin allows those hosts.**

## Two things worth acting on

1. **The On Nut pricing anomaly.** Sukhumvit 77 / On Nut 26 asks ฿31,022/m² on
   the arterial; On Nut 63 asks ฿64,706/m² deeper into a soi. That is a 2.1×
   gap in the same corridor, with the *cheaper* plot holding the better road
   position. One of the two asks is wrong. Finding out which is the highest-value
   question on the register right now, and both are live listings with numbers
   attached — no phone call needed to start, just a look at the two parcels.

2. **Currency check on the Bang Kapi plot.** The source rendered ฿19M with a
   dollar sign. Read as USD it implies ฿385,000/m² — CBD pricing in a mid-city
   district, not credible. Read as baht it is consistent with Bang Kapi, so the
   register records baht and flags it on the card. Worth confirming before the
   number is relied on.
