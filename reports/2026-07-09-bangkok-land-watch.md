# Bangkok Land Watch — 9 July 2026

Snapshot of land plots currently on the market in Bangkok, compiled from
FazWaz, DDproperty, Thailand-Property, Dot Property, and Livinginsider, plus
market benchmarks from Bangkok Post / Nation Thailand / Colliers coverage.

**Market context:** Bangkok median land list price ≈ ฿26.0M; median
฿82,344/sq.wah. Official Treasury appraisals (valid through 2026) top out at
฿1,000,000/sq.wah in the CBD (Silom, Ploenchit, Wireless, Rama I); Ratchadamri
≈ ฿900k, Sathorn ≈ ฿800k, Sukhumvit main road ≈ ฿750k, Phaya Thai ≈ ฿500k,
Lat Phrao ≈ ฿150–200k/sq.wah. Market prices in prime areas transact above
appraisal. Colliers expects CBD land prices to keep rising in 2026, though the
weak condo market is softening prices in condo-dependent locations — a buying
window for well-located plots.

USD figures are as listed on FazWaz; THB conversion approximate (~฿32.5/USD).
Perimeter is **estimated** assuming a near-square plot (4 × √area); confirm
against the Chanote survey.

## District benchmarks for residential development

- **Bang Na** — avg ≈ ฿48,351/m²; plots of 10+ rai still assemblable, the
  go-to for full-scale housing estates in east Bangkok.
- **Krungthep Kreetha / Ramkhamhaeng / Srinakarin** — new Krungthep Kreetha
  road links Rama IX–Ramkhamhaeng–Srinakarin; named a top 2026 hotspot for
  low-rise homes at ฿8–20M.
- **Ram Inthra / Min Buri (Pink Line)** — townhouse & semi-detached demand at
  ฿3–7M around Ramintra and Fashion Island stations.
- **Lat Phrao** — ≈ ¼ the price of Sukhumvit; appraisal ฿150–200k/sq.wah.
- **Outer suburbs generally** — ฿20,000–100,000/m²; small plots start ≈ ฿9.9M.
