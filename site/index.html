Methodology & caveats
  • Perimeter is not published on Thai listings — figures here are estimates assuming a near-square plot (4 × √area). The true boundary comes from the Chanote (title deed) survey drawing.
  • Posted date: most Thai portals hide the original posting date; where unavailable, the register shows the date this tracker first saw the listing.
  • Size gauges & imagery: satellite imagery services are blocked by this workspace's network policy, so each card carries a true-to-scale footprint gauge instead; use the per-plot Google Earth links or the KML download to view real aerial imagery. Anchors are district-level approximations until a seller shares the Chanote location.
  • Spending power is inferred from the residential product that sells in each catchment (e.g. ฿3–7M townhomes on the Pink Line, ฿8–20M houses on Krungthep Kreetha, ฿60M+ ultra-luxury in Phrom Phong).
  • USD listings converted at ≈ ฿32.5/USD. Units: 1 rai = 1,600 m²; 1 sq.wah = 4 m².
  • Sources: FazWaz, DDproperty, Thailand-Property, Dot Property, Livinginsider, Hipflat; market context from Bangkok Post, Nation Thailand, Colliers. Data gathered via web search — portals block direct crawling.
  • Foreign nationals cannot hold Thai land freehold; plan for a majority-Thai company, BOI promotion, or 30-year lease structure.
