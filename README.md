# RE-Thailand — Bangkok Land Watch

Tracking available land plots in Thailand (focus: Bangkok) for a residential
real estate development venture. Each dated report in `reports/` is a snapshot
of plots on the market with price, size, estimated perimeter, and catchment
notes, so changes can be tracked over time and shared with prospective
investors.

## Units cheat-sheet (Thai land measures)

- 1 **rai** = 4 ngan = 400 sq.wah = 1,600 m²
- 1 **ngan** = 100 sq.wah = 400 m²
- 1 **sq.wah (ตารางวา)** = 4 m²
- Listing format `2-2-17.5 rai` = 2 rai, 2 ngan, 17.5 sq.wah

## Notes on the data

- **Perimeter** is not published on listings — portals publish area, and
  sometimes frontage/depth. Reports give an *estimated* perimeter assuming a
  near-square plot (4 × √area). The true figure comes from the Chanote (โฉนด,
  title deed) survey drawing, which the seller provides on request.
- **Catchment** = who the surrounding area sells to: transit lines, demand
  drivers, and the residential product that moves there.
- Foreign nationals cannot own land freehold in Thailand. Typical structures:
  majority-Thai company, BOI promotion, or 30-year registered leasehold.
  Factor this into any investor conversation early.
